    What do these terms mean?
    Tonic
    The home note of the key. Changing it transposes the whole progression.
    Progression
    The order of chords. Roman numerals show where each chord starts relative to the tonic. Uppercase means major, lowercase means minor, ♭ lowers the root, 7 adds a seventh, and ø7 means half-diminished seventh.
    Tempo
    Beats per minute. At 80 BPM, one beat lasts 0.75 seconds.
    Harmonic rhythm
    How long each chord lasts. With 1 · 1 · 2, chords get one beat, one beat, then two beats, repeating from the start of that pattern.
    Playing pattern
    When the notes sound within the beat. An arpeggio plays the notes of a chord one at a time. Patterns with rests can skip a chord if its duration is short.
